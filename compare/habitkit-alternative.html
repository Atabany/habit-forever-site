Looking for a HabitKit alternative?

HabitKit and Habit Forever are close: both show habits as colorful grids, need no account and keep data on your device. Habit Forever may suit you better if you want maximum limits or target ranges, monthly targets, or Siri check-ins with an amount.

Disclosure: we make Habit Forever. HabitKit is the app most similar to ours, and a good one, so this page tries to be straight about where they match, where they differ, and what we could not verify.

If you like HabitKit's look but something is missing, the differences below are what to check. If HabitKit already does everything you need, there is little reason to switch.

What HabitKit does well

HabitKit, by Sebastian Roehl, is a well-known visual habit tracker for iPhone.

  • Grid layouts. Each habit fills in a tile grid over time, so a year of effort fits on one screen.
  • No account, on device. You start tracking immediately and your data stays on your phone.
  • Several habit types. It supports yes/no habits, count habits and amount habits.
  • Flexible pricing. It is free, with a Pro upgrade available monthly, yearly or as a lifetime purchase.

For many people that is exactly right.

What HabitKit and Habit Forever share

  • A colorful grid of each habit's history, in the style of a GitHub contribution graph.
  • No account and on-device storage.
  • Number habits as well as simple check-offs.
  • A free tier and a Pro upgrade with monthly, yearly and lifetime options.

So this is not a choice between a good app and a bad one. It comes down to details.

Limits and ranges

Many habits are about doing less: screen time, coffee, spending. In Habit Forever a number habit can have a minimum, a maximum, or a target range, so "at most 2 hours" counts as a success when you stay under it.

Monthly targets

Some habits do not fit a daily or weekly rhythm, such as calling family or a long hike. Habit Forever lets a habit have a daily, weekly or monthly target, and the streak counts in that period.

Siri with amounts

The "Check In Habit" action works with Siri and Shortcuts and accepts an optional amount and note, so you can log "3 glasses" or "25 minutes" by voice or from an automation.

Habit Forever in brief

Habit Forever is a habit tracker for iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later) by an independent developer. It tracks check-off or number habits with goals, reminders, notes, streaks and colorful day-by-day grids, plus Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and Siri Shortcuts. Each habit shows its current and best streak and consistency, and you can fill in a past day you actually did or archive a habit and keep its history.

There is no account, and habit data stays on your device. It is free for up to three habits; Habit Forever Pro (yearly, monthly or a one-time lifetime purchase) adds unlimited habits, deeper insights such as your best days and time of day, widgets for every habit, and JSON backup export and import. It is rated 4.6★ on the App Store, from 17 ratings.

When to stay with HabitKit

  • You are happy with its grids and habit types and do not need limits, ranges or monthly targets.
  • You have years of history in it and do not want to start again. Habit Forever cannot import HabitKit data; its import only reads its own JSON backups.
  • You prefer its design. With two apps this close, taste is a perfectly good reason.

Habit Forever also has no iCloud sync, no Apple Watch app and no Apple Health integration. If those matter, read our Streaks alternative page.

FAQ

What is a good alternative to HabitKit?

Habit Forever is the closest alternative, with a similar colorful grid, no account and on-device storage, plus maximum limits, target ranges, monthly targets and Siri check-ins with an amount.

Is HabitKit free?

HabitKit is free to use, with a Pro upgrade available as a monthly, yearly or lifetime purchase.

Can I import my HabitKit data into Habit Forever?

No, Habit Forever cannot import HabitKit data and only imports its own JSON backups.

Do HabitKit and Habit Forever need an account?

No, both apps work without an account and keep your habit data on your device.

Does Habit Forever have a lifetime option like HabitKit?

Yes, Habit Forever Pro is available as a one-time lifetime purchase as well as yearly and monthly plans.
