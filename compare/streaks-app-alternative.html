Looking for a Streaks app alternative?

Stay with Streaks if you rely on Apple Watch, Apple Health auto-complete or iCloud sync; it does those very well. Look at an alternative such as Habit Forever if you want number goals with limits, weekly targets, contribution grids, or a free tier to start.

Disclosure: we make Habit Forever, one of the alternatives on this page. We have tried to be fair to Streaks, which is a genuinely excellent app, and to say clearly when it is the better choice.

Streaks is one of the best-known habit trackers on iPhone. The best alternative depends on why you are looking: if it is Apple Watch or Health, there may be no reason to switch at all; if it is the kind of goals you can set, there might be.

What Streaks does well

Streaks, by Crunchy Bagel, has earned its reputation:

  • Pay once. It is a paid upfront app with no in-app purchases and no subscription.
  • Apple Design Award. Its design has been recognised by Apple.
  • Apple Health auto-complete. Habits such as steps or workouts can tick themselves off from your Health data.
  • Apple Watch app. You can check off habits from your wrist.
  • No account, with iCloud sync. Your habits sync across your Apple devices without signing up for anything.
  • A focused list. Its App Store listing describes tracking up to 24 tasks, which keeps the app focused.

When Streaks is the better choice

Honestly, stay with Streaks, or choose it, if:

  • You wear an Apple Watch and want to log habits from it.
  • You want habits to complete automatically from Apple Health, such as a step count or a workout.
  • You use an iPhone and an iPad and want them synced through iCloud.
  • You strongly prefer a single upfront purchase with nothing more to buy.

Habit Forever has no Apple Watch app, no Apple Health integration and no iCloud sync, so it cannot replace Streaks for any of these.

Why people look for an alternative

The reasons we hear most often are about how habits are measured, not about quality:

  • Wanting limits, not just targets: "under 2 hours of screen time" or "spend no more than a set amount".
  • Wanting weekly targets that do not feel like a broken streak on rest days.
  • Wanting to see a long history at a glance as a colored grid.
  • Wanting to try properly for free before paying.

FAQ

What is a good alternative to the Streaks app?

HabitKit and Habit Forever are good alternatives if you want a visual grid without an account, and Habitify suits you if you need Android, Mac or web access.

Is there a free alternative to Streaks?

Yes, Habit Forever is free for up to three habits, and HabitKit and Habitify also have free tiers.

Does Habit Forever work with Apple Watch like Streaks?

No, Habit Forever does not have an Apple Watch app, so Streaks is the better choice if you want to log habits from your wrist.

Can Habit Forever complete habits from Apple Health?

No, Habit Forever does not integrate with Apple Health, while Streaks can complete habits automatically from Health data.

Does Habit Forever have a one-time purchase like Streaks?

Habit Forever Pro is available as a one-time lifetime purchase as well as yearly or monthly plans, after a free tier of up to three habits.
