About Habit Forever

Habit Forever is a habit tracker for iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later) by independent developer Mohamed Elatabany. Track check-off or number habits with goals, reminders, notes, streaks and colorful day-by-day grids, plus Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and Siri Shortcuts. There is no account, and habit data stays on your device. It is free for up to three habits; Habit Forever Pro (yearly, monthly or a one-time lifetime purchase) adds unlimited habits, deeper insights, widgets for every habit and backup export/import. It is rated 5.0★ on the App Store, from 4 ratings, and is currently listed there as “Habit Streak Tracker: Routines”.
