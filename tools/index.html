Free habit tools

Small, free tools that run entirely in your browser. No sign-up, and nothing you type is uploaded.

  • ToolHabit cost calculator

    What a daily coffee, takeaway, cigarettes, subscription or scrolling cost a week, month, year and 10 years, in money and time, and what cutting back saves.

  • ToolHow long until it’s a habit? Calculator

    Enter your start date to see when a new habit typically becomes automatic, based on research: median 66 days, range 18–254 days.

  • ToolHabit grid maker

    Make a GitHub-style year-in-squares grid for any habit. Pick a colour, tap the days you did it or fill from a start date, then download a PNG.

  • ToolPrintable habit tracker

    Make a free printable monthly habit tracker: add your habits, pick a month, then print it or save it as a PDF. No sign-up, nothing uploaded.

  • ToolReading goal calculator

    How many pages a day to read 12, 24 or 52 books a year? Turn books into pages and minutes a day, or back, with your own dates and reading speed.

  • ToolHow much water should I drink a day?

    Free water intake calculator from US National Academies, EFSA and NHS guidance. Adjusts for exercise and heat; shows litres, ounces and glasses.
