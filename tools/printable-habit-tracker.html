How to use a paper habit tracker

  1. Keep the list short. Three to six habits is plenty. A sheet full of empty rows is discouraging; a short list you mostly fill is motivating.
  2. Make each habit specific. “Read 10 pages” is easier to mark than “read more”. If a habit has a number, put it in the name.
  3. Mark it right after you do it. Keep the sheet where the habit happens: on the fridge for water, by the bed for reading.
  4. Missed a day? Leave it blank and carry on. One gap doesn’t undo your progress. Research on habit formation found that missing a single day didn’t materially affect it. See what to do when you break a streak.
  5. Look at the month as a whole. At the end, count the filled squares per row. That number, your consistency, says more than your longest streak.

Paper or an app?

Paper is great when the sheet sits where you’ll see it. It falls short when you’re away from home, when you want reminders, or when a habit is a number you’d like totals for. FAQ

Is this printable habit tracker free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up. Print as many sheets as you like or save them as PDFs.

How do I save the habit tracker as a PDF?

Select Print or save as PDF, then choose Save as PDF as the destination in your browser’s print dialog. On iPhone, tap Print, then use the share button in the print preview to save it to Files.

How many habits fit on one page?

Up to 12 habits fit on one landscape page with a column for every day of the month.

Is my habit list uploaded anywhere?

No. The sheet is built in your browser and nothing is sent to a server or stored.
