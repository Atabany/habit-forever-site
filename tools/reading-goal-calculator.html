Reading goal calculator

To read 12 books a year, read about 10 pages a day, or roughly 11 minutes at an average adult reading speed; 24 books takes about 20 pages (23 minutes) and 52 books about 43 pages (49 minutes). That assumes 300-page books with 275 words a page. Change any number below and the others follow.

Book length and reading speed

238 words a minute is the average adult silent reading rate for non-fiction in Brysbaert’s 2019 review; fiction averaged 260. To find your own, read for 5 minutes, count the pages, and multiply by your words per page.

How it works

  • Reading days = days from the start date to the finish date, counting both, × days you read each week ÷ 7.
  • Pages a day = books × pages per book ÷ reading days.
  • Minutes a day = pages a day × words per page ÷ words a minute.

The 300 pages per book and 275 words per page are my assumptions, not measured averages: books vary a lot, so change them to match what you read. A dense paperback can hold far more words on a page than a large-print or illustrated book. The reading speed comes from research: Marc Brysbaert’s 2019 meta-analysis of 190 studies with 18,573 participants put adult silent reading in English at 238 words a minute for non-fiction and 260 for fiction. The often-quoted 300 words a minute is higher than what most people read.

Make the goal a daily habit

A yearly number is easy to set and easy to forget. A small daily number is the one you can act on tonight: 10 pages before bed is a book a month. Tie it to something you already do, like after dinner or on the train, and count pages rather than books, so a slow week with a long book still shows progress. More ideas in how to build a reading habit. If you miss a day, carry on the next; one gap doesn’t undo a habit, as I explain in what a broken streak really means.

FAQ

How many pages a day do I need to read 52 books a year?

About 43 pages a day, or roughly 49 minutes at 238 words a minute, if your books average 300 pages with 275 words a page. Shorter books or a faster reading speed bring that down.

How many pages a day is 12 books a year?

About 10 pages a day, roughly 11 minutes, with 300-page books. That is one book a month.

What is the average reading speed?

Brysbaert’s 2019 review of 190 studies found that adults read English silently at about 238 words a minute for non-fiction and 260 for fiction. Your own speed depends on the text and why you’re reading it.

Do audiobooks count?

That’s your call. In the calculator, set the reading speed to the narrator’s pace instead, or track minutes rather than pages.
