Privacy policy

Habit Forever (com.atabany.habitforever on Google Play) is made by Mohamed Elatabany, who is also the data controller. Contact: atabany.apps@gmail.com.

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your habits stay on your phone. The only thing the app sends is what a purchase needs.

Your habits

Your habits, check-ins, amounts, notes, reminders and settings are stored on your phone. There is no account and no server of mine that receives them. Reminders are scheduled on the phone, and widgets, notifications, the Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts read the same data on the phone.

Copies leave the phone only where you send them:

  • Your Google backup. If backup is on in your phone’s settings, Android may include the app’s habits and settings in the backup kept in your own Google account. You control it in your phone’s settings, and Google’s privacy policy applies.
  • A backup folder. If you choose a folder, the app writes a backup file there. If that folder belongs to a cloud service, that service stores the file.
  • Export and sharing. Export files and share pictures go to the app you pick.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play; I never see your payment details. The app uses RevenueCat to check subscriptions and the lifetime purchase. RevenueCat receives the purchase (product, order, status and dates), an anonymous ID created by the app, and basic device information such as the app version, the Android version and the phone’s language and region. It also receives your IP address, as any internet connection does. I use this to unlock Pro, to restore it on another phone, and to see how many subscriptions there are. It isn’t linked to your name or email, isn’t sold, and isn’t used for advertising. RevenueCat acts for me under its privacy policy, and the connection is encrypted.

Permissions

  • Notifications and alarms: to show your reminders and the timer. Exact alarms are optional.
  • Start when the phone starts: to set your reminders again after a restart.
  • Internet: for purchases only.
  • Vibration: for a short touch when you check in.

The app doesn’t ask for your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data.

Keeping and deleting

Delete a habit in the app, or uninstall the app, and its data on the phone is gone. Remove copies in your Google backup or folder where they are kept. To have the purchase record tied to your anonymous ID deleted at RevenueCat, email me: deleting it doesn’t cancel a subscription, so cancel in Google Play first. Google keeps its own record of your orders.
