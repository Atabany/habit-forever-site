Effective date: October 22, 2026. This policy covers the Habit Forever app for iPhone and iPad (“Habit Forever: Streak Tracker” on the App Store, formerly “Habit Streak Tracker: Routines”) and this website. The developer and data controller is Mohamed Elatabany (“I”). Contact: atabany.apps@gmail.com.

Your habit data stays on your device

Habits, check-ins, amounts, notes, reminders and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account, and I don’t operate a server that receives them. The app doesn’t sync them through iCloud. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts read the same on-device data. Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device.

If you use backup export (Pro), the app creates a file and you decide where it goes. I never receive it unless you send it to me.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple; I never see your payment details. The app uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and the lifetime purchase. To do that, RevenueCat receives purchase information (such as the product, transaction and its status) together with an anonymous app user ID generated by the app, along with basic device information such as the app version and operating system. This information isn’t linked to your name or email, and it isn’t used for advertising. It appears in the App Store privacy label as Purchases and Identifiers, “not linked to you”.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking across apps or websites. It doesn’t request your location, contacts or photos.

Support emails

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you include, and use it only to reply. You can ask me to delete our correspondence at any time.

This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. The free tools run entirely in your browser and send nothing anywhere. Links to the App Store include a campaign tag so Apple can tell me, in aggregate, which page a download came from; Apple does not share personal data with me.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete any habit or entry in the app, or delete the app to remove all its data from the device.
  • Export your data (Pro) whenever you like.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data. Because I hold almost nothing about you, most requests are answered by the controls above; for anything else, email me.

Children

The app isn’t directed to children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update the effective date above and describe significant changes in the app’s release notes.
