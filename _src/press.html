How to describe it

In one line: Habit Forever is a private habit tracker for iPhone that turns every day you show up into a colored square.

In about 50 words: Habit Forever is a habit tracker for iPhone and iPad. Check in with one tap or log an amount, reach a number or stay under a limit, and watch each habit fill a colorful grid of days. There’s no account, data stays on your device, and it’s free for three habits.

Longer:

What it does

  • Two kinds of habit. Check-off habits (done or not), or number habits counted in pages, glasses, minutes, distance, money or a unit you name.
  • Four kinds of goal. Check it off, reach a number (8 glasses, 20 pages), stay under a limit for things you want less of (2 hours, 2 coffees), or just track a reading such as weight or mood. Goals are per day or per week, on every day, weekdays or chosen days, and days off never break a streak.
  • Progress you can see. Current and best streak, a consistency figure, and a colorful day-by-day grid for each habit, similar to a GitHub contribution graph. Milestone celebrations.
  • Three Today layouts. Grid, Board (months of days for each habit) and Week.
  • Reminders for each habit that stay quiet on days you’ve already checked in.
  • Notes on any check-in, and you can fill in a missed day from the last seven days. Archive a habit and keep its history.
  • Widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen that let you check in without opening the app.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: Check In Habit (with an optional amount and note), Get Habit Streak and Open Habit.
  • Light and dark themes, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver.

Free includes up to three habits with check-off and number habits, goals and limits, reminders, notes, streaks and grids, and a widget for your first habit. Pro adds unlimited habits, deeper insights (best days, time of day, month by month), widgets for every habit and backup export and import.

What it doesn’t do

To keep descriptions accurate: Habit Forever has no Apple Watch app, no Apple Health integration, no iCloud sync, no Android, Mac or web version, no social features or challenges, and no AI features. If someone needs those, our comparison says which app fits better.

Questions

Who makes Habit Forever?

Mohamed Elatabany, an independent iOS developer. There is no company, investor or ad network behind it.

Is Habit Forever free?

Yes, for up to three habits with no time limit. Habit Forever Pro, sold yearly, monthly or as a one-time lifetime purchase, unlocks unlimited habits, deeper insights, widgets for every habit and backup export and import.

Does Habit Forever need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or sign-in, and habit data is stored on your device.

Is Habit Forever the same app as Habit Streak Tracker: Routines?

Yes. The app was renamed to Habit Forever in version 4.0 in October 2026. It is the same app from the same developer, and existing data carried over.
