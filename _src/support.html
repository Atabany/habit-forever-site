Purchases and subscriptions

How do I restore Habit Forever Pro on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with, open the app, open the Pro screen and tap Restore. Your subscription or lifetime purchase is linked to your Apple Account, not to the device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Habit Forever and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Sign in, choose the purchase and select Request a refund.

Your data

Where are my habits stored?

On your device. Habit Forever has no account and no server copy of your habits, check-ins or notes, and it doesn’t sync through iCloud.

How do I move my habits to a new iPhone?

With Pro, open Settings in the app and tap Export backup. Send the file to your new device (AirDrop, Files or email), then tap Import backup in the same Settings screen. A full device-to-device iPhone transfer or an encrypted iPhone backup also carries the app’s data.

I deleted the app. Can I get my habits back?

Only from a backup. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. If you exported a backup or have an iPhone backup from before the deletion, you can restore from it.

Reminders and widgets

My reminders don’t arrive.

Check Settings → Notifications → Habit Forever and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Then check that a Focus mode isn’t silencing the app, and that the habit’s reminder is set for the days you expect.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Habit Forever. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen and tap the widget area. The guide to habit tracker widgets has step-by-step instructions.
