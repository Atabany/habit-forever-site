A habit tracker widget puts today's habits where you already look dozens of times a day. To add one, touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit at the top, tap Add Widget, and choose your habit app. Lock Screen widgets are added from the Customize screen instead.

The exact wording has shifted a little between iOS versions, so the steps below cover iOS 17, iOS 18 and iOS 26.

Add a widget to the Home Screen

iOS 18 and iOS 26

  1. Go to the Home Screen page where you want the widget.
  2. Touch and hold the background until the apps begin to jiggle.
  3. Tap Edit at the top of the screen, then tap Add Widget.
  4. Scroll or search for your habit tracker, tap it, then swipe through the sizes and styles.
  5. When you see the size you want, tap Add Widget.
  6. While the apps are still jiggling, drag the widget into place, then tap Done.

iOS 17

The steps are the same, except that after the apps jiggle you tap the add button (the plus sign) at the top of the screen to open the widget gallery.

On newer iOS versions the Edit control may appear as a button icon rather than the word "Edit". It sits in the same place at the top of the screen.

Tip: to change which habit a widget shows, touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose a habit. You can also stack widgets on top of each other to save space.

Add a widget to the Lock Screen

Lock Screen widgets have been available since iOS 16 and are small and monochrome: a circle, a rectangle, or a single line of text above the clock.

  1. Wake your iPhone and unlock it with Face ID or Touch ID, but stay on the Lock Screen.
  2. Touch and hold the Lock Screen until the Customize button appears, then tap it.
  3. If you are asked to choose, pick the Lock Screen.
  4. Tap Add Widgets, or tap the widget area below the time.
  5. Tap or drag the habit widget you want. If there is no room, remove another widget first.
  6. Close the widget list, then tap Done.

The single-line widget lives in the spot next to the date above the clock; tap the date while customizing to change it.

What makes a good habit widget

Not all habit widgets are equal. Before you choose an app for its widget, check three things:

  • Can you check in from the widget? Since iOS 17, widgets can run small actions without opening the app. A widget that only displays your streak still makes you open the app to log.
  • Does it work on the Lock Screen? A Lock Screen widget is visible every time you pick up your phone, even before you unlock it.
  • Is it free? Some apps limit widgets to paid plans. Check before you build your routine around one.

Habit Forever's widgets

We make Habit Forever, a habit tracker for iPhone and iPad, so here is exactly what its widgets do.

The Habit widget (one habit)

  • Home Screen sizes: small and medium. It shows one habit's streak, its week and recent activity.

The All Habits widget

Medium and large sizes show your whole day at a glance so you can check in on your habits without opening the app; the large size also shows how many of today's habits are done. It is part of Habit Forever Pro.

Free and Pro

  • Free: the Habit widget works for your first habit, on both the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.

If your habit widget is not updating

  • Open the app once after installing or updating it. Widgets read data the app has saved.
  • Check that the habit is due today. A habit scheduled for other days will not offer a check-in.
  • Remove and re-add the widget if it shows an old habit after you renamed or archived it.
  • Turn off Low Power Mode briefly. iOS may refresh widgets less often to save battery.

FAQ

Can I check off a habit from the iPhone Lock Screen?

Yes, on iOS 17 or later a Lock Screen widget with a button can log a habit without opening the app, and Habit Forever's rectangular Lock Screen widget includes a check-in button.

Why can't I find my habit app in the widget gallery?

Open the app at least once after installing it, then search for it by name in the widget gallery, because widgets only appear after the app has run.

How do I change which habit a widget shows?

Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose a different habit from the list.

Are Habit Forever's widgets free?

The Habit widget is free for your first habit on both the Home Screen and Lock Screen, while widgets for every habit and the All Habits widget come with Habit Forever Pro.

Can a widget log a number, like glasses of water?

Yes, in Habit Forever a tap on the widget logs the fixed amount you chose for a single tap in that habit, such as one glass.
