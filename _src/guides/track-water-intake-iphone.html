To track water intake on iPhone without installing anything, open the Health app, find Water in the Nutrition category, and tap Add Data to log each drink. If you would rather count glasses toward a daily goal and keep a streak, a habit tracker with number habits is quicker day to day.

Here are both methods, step by step, with the honest pros and cons of each.

Method 1: Log water in the Apple Health app

The Health app comes with every iPhone and has a Water data type in its Nutrition category.

  1. Open the Health app.
  2. Go to Browse. On iOS 17 and 18 it is a tab at the bottom of the screen; on newer versions, use the browse or search button at the bottom right.
  3. Tap Nutrition, then tap Water. You can also simply search for "Water".
  4. Tap Add Data in the top-right corner.
  5. Enter the amount, check the date and time, and tap Add.

To see your water on the Summary screen every day, scroll down on the Water page and tap Pin to Summary. You can change the unit (for example milliliters or fluid ounces) under Unit on the same page.

Pros and cons of the Health app

  • Pros: free and built in; charts by day, week, month and year; other apps you allow can add water data too.
  • Cons: logging takes several taps every time; there is no daily goal to hit, no streak, and no reminder to drink.

The Health app is a good record. It is not designed to help you build the habit of drinking more.

Why track water as a habit instead

Most people who want to track water really want to drink more water, consistently. That is a habit problem, and habit tools help with it:

  • A daily target, such as 8 glasses, so you know when you are done.
  • One-tap logging from the Home Screen or Lock Screen, so a glass takes one second to record.
  • Reminders at times that suit you, which stop once you have hit the target.
  • A streak and a grid, so a good week is visible and worth protecting.

Method 2: Track water in Habit Forever

We make Habit Forever, a habit tracker for iPhone and iPad. Water is one of its starter habits, so setup takes seconds.

  1. Tap to add a habit and choose Drink water from the suggestions. It starts as 8 glasses a day, with one tap adding one glass.
  2. Or build your own: pick Reach a number as the goal, set the amount (for example 8) and the unit (glasses, ml, or anything you like).
  3. Set the amount a single tap adds, such as one glass, so tapping the habit logs it instantly.
  4. Add a reminder or two, for example mid-morning and mid-afternoon. Reminders only arrive on the habit's days and never once it is done.

Log a glass in one tap

  • In the app: tap the habit to add a glass, or open it to type a different amount.
  • From a widget: add the Habit widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen and tap its check-in button. Each tap logs your one-tap amount. See how to add a habit tracker widget.
  • With Siri or Shortcuts: the "Check In Habit" action accepts an optional amount and note, so you can log 2 glasses by voice or build a Shortcut for your water bottle. See how to log habits with Siri and Shortcuts.

See your progress

Each habit shows your current and best streak, your consistency, and a colorful day-by-day grid. Habit Forever Pro adds deeper insights, such as your best days and time of day.

One thing to know: Habit Forever does not connect to Apple Health. Water you log in the app is not written to Health, and water logged in Health does not appear in the app. If you need your water in Health, for example for a nutrition app that reads it, use Method 1 as well, or choose an app that writes to Health.

Tips for drinking more water

  • Tie it to a cue. A glass after waking, with each meal and after brushing your teeth already gets you to four.
  • Use the same glass or bottle so your unit means something.
  • Start below your goal. If you drink 3 glasses now, aim for 5 before 8.
  • Ask a professional about your needs. How much water you need depends on your body, activity and climate.

FAQ

Does iPhone have a built-in water tracker?

Yes, the Health app has a Water data type under Nutrition where you can tap Add Data to log each drink, although it has no daily goal or reminders.

Can I log water from my iPhone Lock Screen?

Yes, with a habit tracker that has interactive Lock Screen widgets, such as Habit Forever, you can tap the widget to log a glass without opening the app.

Does Habit Forever sync water with Apple Health?

No, Habit Forever does not integrate with Apple Health, so water logged in the app stays in the app.

Can I log water with Siri?

Yes, Habit Forever's Check In Habit action for Siri and Shortcuts accepts an optional amount, so you can log more than one glass at a time.

Can I track water in milliliters instead of glasses?

Yes, in Habit Forever you can set any unit for a number habit, including milliliters or ounces.
