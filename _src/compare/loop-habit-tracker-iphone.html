There is no official Loop Habit Tracker for iPhone. Loop is a free, open-source Android app, distributed through Google Play and F-Droid, and its developer has not released an iOS version.

If you used Loop on Android and have moved to iPhone, or a friend recommended it, here is what Loop does well and which iPhone apps come closest.

What Loop Habit Tracker is

Loop Habit Tracker is a well-loved habit tracker for Android. It is free and its source code is public on GitHub. You can install it from F-Droid or Google Play.

People like it because it is simple, private and honest about progress. Its history views show your check-ins as a calendar grid, so you can see months of effort at once.

A note on apps with the same name

If you search the App Store for "Loop habit", you may see an app that uses a similar name. As far as we can tell, it is not made by Loop's developer and is not the open-source Android app. Check the developer name on any listing before you assume it is the same project.

What to look for in an iPhone replacement

Most Loop users care about the same few things:

  • No account. Loop never asked you to sign up.
  • A history grid. A calendar or heatmap view of your check-ins.
  • Flexible schedules, such as three times a week.
  • Number habits, not just yes or no.
  • A fair price, ideally with a one-time option.

No iPhone app is open source and free in exactly the way Loop is, so expect to make some trade-off.

HabitKit

HabitKit is probably the closest in spirit: no account, data on the device, and grid layouts where each habit fills in tiles over time. It supports yes/no, count and amount habits, and is free with a Pro upgrade. Read our HabitKit alternative page for how it compares with Habit Forever.

Streaks

Streaks is a polished, Apple Design Award-winning app you buy once, with no account and iCloud sync. It has an Apple Watch app and can complete habits automatically from Apple Health. If you want sync between your iPhone and iPad, it is a strong pick. See Streaks app alternative.

Habit Forever

We make Habit Forever, so take this with that in mind. It shares several things Loop users tend to like:

  • No account, and habit records and notes stay on your device.
  • A colorful day-by-day grid for each habit, in the style of a GitHub contribution graph, plus current and best streak and consistency.
  • Flexible goals: daily, weekly or monthly targets, on every day or chosen days.
  • Number habits with a minimum, a maximum for things you want less of, or a target range.
  • Widgets and Siri: check in from the Home Screen or Lock Screen, or with the "Check In Habit" action.

It differs from Loop in important ways. It is not open source. It is free for up to three habits, and Habit Forever Pro (yearly, monthly or a one-time lifetime purchase) unlocks unlimited habits, deeper insights, widgets for every habit and JSON backup export and import. It has no Android version, so it will not help if you still use an Android phone too.

Can you move your Loop data to iPhone?

To our knowledge, none of the apps above advertise an import from Loop. Habit Forever cannot import Loop data. Its import only reads its own JSON backups.

The practical route is to start fresh. If you want to keep your old record, save whatever export Loop offers on your Android phone, or take screenshots of your history. If a recent run matters to you, you can fill in past days in Habit Forever by tapping them in the habit's grid, but only do this for days you actually did.

A fresh start is not a loss. Habit research suggests that progress lives in the repetitions you have already made, not in a number on a screen; see what a broken streak really means.

FAQ

Is Loop Habit Tracker available on iPhone?

No, Loop Habit Tracker is an Android-only app available on Google Play and F-Droid, and it has no official iOS version.

What is the closest iPhone app to Loop Habit Tracker?

HabitKit and Habit Forever are close in approach, because both work without an account, keep data on the device and show habits as a visual grid.

Is there a free open-source habit tracker for iPhone like Loop?

We are not aware of an open-source iPhone tracker as established as Loop, but several apps, including HabitKit and Habit Forever, have free tiers.

Can I import my Loop Habit Tracker data into Habit Forever?

No, Habit Forever does not support importing Loop data and can only import its own JSON backups.

Is the app called Loop on the App Store the same as Loop Habit Tracker?

Loop Habit Tracker's developer has not released an official iOS app, so check the developer name on any App Store listing that uses a similar name.
