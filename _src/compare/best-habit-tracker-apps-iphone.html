How to choose

  • Do you wear an Apple Watch or want Health data to tick habits off? Streaks is the strongest choice here.
  • Do you use Android, a Mac or the web too? Habitify works across all of them, with an account.
  • Do you want no account at all? Streaks, HabitKit and Habit Forever all work without one.
  • Do you track numbers, not just done or not done? HabitKit, Strides and Habit Forever all handle amounts.
  • Does a game keep you going? Habitica turns habits into a role-playing game.

Streaks

Streaks, by Crunchy Bagel, is a long-standing favorite and an Apple Design Award winner. You pay once upfront, with no in-app purchases. It needs no account and syncs through iCloud. Habits can be completed automatically from Apple Health data, and it has an Apple Watch app. Its App Store listing describes tracking up to 24 tasks.

Choose it if you live in Apple's ecosystem and want habits like steps or workouts to complete themselves. See our Streaks alternative page for a closer comparison.

HabitKit

HabitKit, by Sebastian Roehl, is known for its grid layouts, where each habit fills in a tile pattern over time. It runs without an account, with data on the device, and supports yes/no, count and amount habits. It is free with a Pro upgrade available monthly, yearly or as a lifetime purchase.

Choose it if you want a clean, visual tracker. It is the closest app to Habit Forever; see our HabitKit alternative page.

Habitify

Habitify runs on iOS, Android, Mac and the web, with an account (including Sign in with Apple) and cloud sync. It groups habits into areas and connects to Zapier and IFTTT. It is free with a subscription or lifetime upgrade.

Choose it if you switch between devices that are not all Apple, or want automations.

(Not Boring) Habits

(Not Boring) Habits is a design-led tracker with a playful 3D style and an Apple Design Award. It is subscription-based with limited free use.

Choose it if how an app feels matters as much as what it does.

Way of Life

Way of Life keeps things simple: each day you mark a habit done, not done, or skipped. It is free with in-app purchases.

Choose it if you want quick yes/no logging and do not care about counting.

Habitica

Habitica turns your habits into a role-playing game: you level up a character and can join parties with friends. It is open source and requires an account.

Choose it if plain checklists bore you and social accountability helps.

Everyday

Everyday shows habits on a colorful calendar. It uses an account with web sync and offers a subscription or a lifetime purchase.

Choose it if you want a bright monthly calendar view and web access.

Strides

Strides combines habits with longer goals and offers four tracker types: habit, target, average and milestone. It is free with in-app purchases.

Choose it if you want to track a savings goal or a weight target next to daily habits.

Habit Forever

Habit Forever is our app, for iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 or later. It tracks check-off habits or number habits such as pages, glasses, minutes, distance or spending. Goals can be a minimum, a maximum for habits you want less of, or a target range, and can be daily, weekly or monthly, on every day or chosen days. Each habit has a current and best streak, a consistency figure, and a colorful day-by-day contribution grid. You can check in from Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, or with Siri and Shortcuts.

There is no account, and habit data stays on your device. It is free for up to three habits; Habit Forever Pro (yearly, monthly or a one-time lifetime purchase) adds unlimited habits, deeper insights, widgets for every habit and JSON backup export and import. It is rated 5.0★ on the App Store, from 4 ratings, so it is still a young app.

Where others are better: Habit Forever has no Apple Watch app, no Apple Health integration, no iCloud sync, no Android or web version, and no social features. If you need any of those, Streaks, Habitify or Habitica will serve you better.

Choose it if you track numbers and limits, want weekly or monthly targets, and prefer no account.

FAQ

What is the best habit tracker app for iPhone?

It depends on your needs: Streaks suits Apple Watch and Health users, Habitify suits people on several platforms, Habitica suits people who like games, and HabitKit and Habit Forever suit people who want a visual grid without an account.

Which habit tracker works with Apple Watch?

Streaks has an Apple Watch app and can complete habits automatically from Apple Health data, while Habit Forever does not have an Apple Watch app.

Which habit trackers do not need an account?

Streaks, HabitKit and Habit Forever all work without creating an account.

Is there a free habit tracker for iPhone?

Several apps have free tiers, including HabitKit, Habitify, Way of Life, Strides and Habit Forever, which is free for up to three habits.

Which habit tracker is a one-time purchase?

Streaks is paid upfront with no in-app purchases, and HabitKit, Habitify, Everyday and Habit Forever offer a lifetime option alongside their subscriptions.
