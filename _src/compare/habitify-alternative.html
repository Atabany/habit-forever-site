What Habitify does well

Habitify, by Unstatic Ltd Co, is one of the most complete habit trackers available.

  • Every platform. Its App Store listing covers iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Apple Vision, and its website adds Android and a web app.
  • Sync. Your habits sync across devices through your account, including Sign in with Apple.
  • Integrations. The App Store listing says it can log progress automatically from Apple Health, Google Health/Fit, Fitbit, Strava and Screen Time or RescueTime, with your consent. Its pricing page lists Apple and Google calendar integrations, an API, and automation through Zapier and IFTTT on its paid plan.
  • A full toolkit. Time-of-day sections for your routine, a built-in timer, notes, mood tracking, challenges with friends, Siri support, and reminders that can follow a habit stack or a location.
  • Languages. The App Store lists English and 39 other languages.

On pricing, Habitify's pricing page shows a free plan (listed at 3 habits, with cross-platform sync) and a Premium plan billed annually or once as a lifetime purchase. The App Store listing shows Free, Plus and Pro tiers with monthly, yearly, lifetime and family purchases, and a recent release note says free users can now create more habits, with those beyond the basic limit view-only. The two sources describe the plans differently, so check in the app for what applies to you.

When Habitify is the better choice

  • You use Android, a Mac, the web or an Apple Watch, or switch between devices. Habit Forever runs only on iPhone and iPad and has no sync.
  • You want habits to complete themselves from Apple Health, Fitbit, Strava or Screen Time.
  • You want Zapier, IFTTT, calendar integration or an API.
  • You want a timer, mood tracking or challenges with friends inside your habit tracker.
  • You need the app in a language other than English. Habit Forever is in English only.

If any of those matter, stay with Habitify. There is no point switching for a smaller feature set.

Why some people look for an alternative

Common reasons are about simplicity and ownership: not wanting an account for a personal checklist, preferring data on the phone rather than a server, wanting to pay once rather than subscribe, or wanting fewer features and a calmer screen. Those are the areas where Habit Forever is different.

Where Habit Forever fits

Habit Forever is a habit tracker for iPhone and iPad that I build on my own. It does fewer things than Habitify on purpose.

No account, on your device

You open it and start. There is no sign-up, and habit records and notes are stored on your device. The trade-off is real: there is no sync, so it is a poor fit if you track on several devices. For more on that trade-off, read how to choose a private habit tracker with no account.

Numbers, limits and weekly goals

A habit can be a simple check-off or a number in any unit. Goals can be a minimum ("at least 20 pages"), or a maximum for habits you want less of ("at most 2 cups of coffee"), per day or per week. A weekly goal counts its streak in weeks. See weekly habit goals and how to track a habit you want to do less.

Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

"Check in on Habit Forever" works with Siri as soon as the app is installed, and the Check In Habit action in Shortcuts takes an optional amount and note, so you can log from the Action button or an automation. See how to log habits with Siri and Shortcuts. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets let you check in without opening the app.

Free, or pay once

It is free for up to three habits with no time limit. Habit Forever Pro adds unlimited habits, deeper insights such as your strongest days and time of day, widgets for every habit, and JSON backup export and import, as a yearly or monthly plan or a one-time lifetime purchase. If you switch

Habit Forever cannot import Habitify data; its import only reads its own backups. The simplest path is to start your main habits fresh and keep Habitify's history for reference. If you have years of history and use more than one device, that is a good reason to stay.

FAQ

What is a good alternative to Habitify?

If you only use iPhone or iPad, Habit Forever is an alternative with no account, data on your device, limits and weekly goals, and a one-time lifetime option; if you need Android, Mac, web or sync, Habitify remains the better fit.

Does Habitify work on Android and Mac?

Yes, Habitify's website lists iOS, Android and a web app, and its App Store listing covers iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Apple Vision.

Does Habit Forever need an account like Habitify?

No, Habit Forever works without an account and keeps your habit data on your device, with no sync between devices.

Can I import my Habitify data into Habit Forever?

No, Habit Forever cannot import Habitify data and only imports its own backups.

Does Habitify have a lifetime option?

Yes, Habitify's pricing page and App Store listing both show a lifetime purchase alongside its other plans.
