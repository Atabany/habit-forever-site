How to track a "3 times a week" habit

To track a habit you want to do 3 times a week, give it a weekly goal instead of a daily one: the week counts as done once you reach 3, on whichever days suit you, and the streak counts weeks. If your days are fixed, such as Monday, Wednesday and Friday, schedule only those days so the others never count as misses.

The gym, a long run, calling your parents, cooking at home: plenty of good habits are not daily. Track them with a daily checklist and every rest day looks like a failure, and the streak resets for no reason. A weekly goal fixes that. Here is how to set one up with what is already on your iPhone, and then how Habit Forever counts it.

First, decide which kind of weekly habit it is

  • Fixed days. You run on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. The habit belongs to those days.
  • Flexible days. You want three workouts a week, whenever they fit. Tuesday, Saturday and Sunday counts just as well.

Flexible goals are kinder to a busy life, because a missed Monday can still be made up on Thursday. Fixed days are easier to plan around and to attach reminders to. Neither is better; pick the one that matches how your week really goes.

With built-in apps

Reminders handles fixed days well. Create a reminder such as "Run", open its details, set a time and choose a custom repeat on the weekdays you want. It comes back on those days and you tick it off. What it cannot do is flexible days: there is no way to say "any three days this week", and it keeps no count of how many weeks you managed.

Paper handles flexible days well. Draw one row per week and make a tally mark each time you go; three marks and the week is done. Our printable habit tracker works for this if you treat each row as a week. What paper cannot do is remind you on Thursday that you still have two to go.

Flexible days in Habit Forever: a weekly goal

I built weekly goals into Habit Forever because "3 times a week" is one of the most natural goals there is. To set one up:

  1. Tap to add a new habit and give it a name, such as "Run".
  2. Under Goal, choose Reach a number.
  3. Set it to at least 3, type or pick a unit such as "times" or "runs", and choose Each week.
  4. Turn on One tap adds and set it to 1, so each tap of the habit's circle logs one session without a keypad.

There is also a ready-made "Run: 3 runs a week" idea in the new habit list that fills all of this in for you.

During the week, the habit shows how far along you are this week. Once you reach 3, the week counts as done, however the sessions were spread.

How the streak counts

For a habit with a weekly goal, the streak is counted in weeks, not days. Each week in which you reached your goal adds one; a week in which you fell short ends the streak. A few details matter:

  • The current week is never held against you. On Wednesday with one session logged, your streak is not broken; the week is not over yet. Only a finished week that fell short breaks it.
  • You choose when a week starts. In Settings, "Week starts on" can be Monday, Sunday or Saturday, and the weekly count follows it.
  • The best streak is in weeks too, and the consistency figure becomes the share of recent weeks in which you reached the goal (more on that in tracking consistency instead of streaks).
  • On the day-by-day grid, a week you completed is filled in as a whole week, so a run of good weeks reads as one solid band of color. You can draw a grid like this for any habit with the free habit grid maker.

Siri tells you the same thing: ask "What's my streak in Habit Forever" and the answer comes in weeks. See how to log habits with Siri and Shortcuts.

Fixed days in Habit Forever: a schedule

If your habit belongs to specific days, keep it a simple check-off habit and change its Days from Every day to Weekdays, or tap Choose and pick the days. On the other days the habit shows as a rest day. As the app puts it: days off never break a streak. A Monday, Wednesday, Friday habit with all three done this week and last week is a streak of six, not a broken chain.

Reminders follow the habit's days too, and stay quiet once you have checked in.

Weekly limits for things you want less of

A weekly goal also works as a limit. "Stay under" with "Each week" suits habits that are fine in moderation but add up: takeaway meals, hours of gaming, money spent on a hobby. The week counts as a success as long as your total stays at or under the limit. See how to track a habit you want to do less for more on limits.

New habits in Habit Forever take a daily or a weekly goal, not a monthly one. For something you do about once a month, such as paying a bill or a deep clean, a monthly repeating reminder in the Reminders app is the simpler tool.

Tips for a weekly habit that lasts

  1. Set the goal you can hit in a bad week. If you sometimes manage four workouts and sometimes two, set three, or even two. A streak of hit weeks is worth more than an ambitious goal you miss half the time.
  2. Plan your days on Sunday. A flexible goal still needs a plan; decide which days you will aim for, and treat the others as back-ups.
  3. Do not save it all for the weekend. If the week is half gone and you have done none, do a short session today rather than hoping for three on Saturday.
  4. Expect it to take time. Automaticity grows with repetitions, so a weekly habit usually takes longer to feel automatic than a daily one; see how long it takes to form a habit.

Missed a week? It happens. Read what to do after a missed day; the same advice applies to a missed week.

FAQ

How do I track a habit 3 times a week?

Give it a weekly goal of 3 so the week counts as done once you reach 3 on any days, or, if your days are fixed, schedule the habit only on those days.

Does a weekly habit break my streak on rest days?

No, in Habit Forever a habit with a weekly goal counts its streak in weeks, and the current week is never held against you until it ends.

Can I choose which day my week starts on?

Yes, in Habit Forever's Settings, "Week starts on" can be Monday, Sunday or Saturday, and weekly goals follow it.

Can the Reminders app track a 3 times a week habit?

Reminders can repeat on fixed weekdays, but it cannot count "any three days this week" or show how many weeks you managed.
