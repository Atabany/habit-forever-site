How to build a reading habit

To build a reading habit, set a small daily minimum in pages or minutes, attach it to a fixed moment such as after dinner, and track it. Twelve books a year of about 300 pages works out to roughly 10 pages a day, which at an average reading speed is about 10 to 13 minutes.

"Read more" is one of the most common resolutions, and one of the easiest to drop, because it is not a habit yet; it is a wish. What turns it into a habit is a number small enough to hit on a tired day and a moment in the day it belongs to. I built number habits into Habit Forever for goals like this one, and this guide shows the math, the built-in options on iPhone, and how to set it up.

From books per year to pages per day

Start from the goal you want and work backwards. Book lengths vary a lot, so the table assumes an average of 300 pages per book; swap in your own number if you read shorter or longer books.

Books per yearPages per year (300-page books)Pages per day
61,800about 5
12 (one a month)3,600about 10
24 (two a month)7,200about 20
52 (one a week)15,600about 43

The formula is simple: books per year × pages per book ÷ 365. A book a month is just 10 pages a day. That is the surprising part for most people: the gap between "I never read" and "I read a book a month" is a quarter of an hour. How long does a page take?

A 2019 meta-analysis by Marc Brysbaert, covering 190 studies and 18,573 participants, put the average silent reading rate of adults in English at about 238 words per minute for non-fiction and 260 for fiction. Most adults read English non-fiction at somewhere between 175 and 300 words per minute, and the paper notes that these estimates are lower than the figures often cited in scientific and popular writing.

How many words fit on a page depends on the book. If a page holds roughly 250 to 300 words, a page takes about one minute to a minute and a quarter at those speeds. So:

  • 10 pages a day is roughly 10 to 13 minutes.
  • 20 pages a day is roughly 20 to 25 minutes.

These are averages. Dense non-fiction, a second language or a book you are studying will be slower, and that is fine.

Pages or minutes?

  • Pages are easy to count and give a clear sense of progress through a book. They are unfair to dense books: 10 pages of philosophy is not 10 pages of a thriller.
  • Minutes treat every book the same and suit audiobooks. They are harder to keep honest if you read with your phone nearby.

Pick one and stay with it for a month. If you switch between hard and easy books a lot, minutes are usually the better choice.

Built-in options on iPhone

Apple Books has Reading Goals: it tracks how much you read in a day, your streak and how many books you have read this year, and it counts reading you do in the Books app. If you read e-books or audiobooks there, it is a good free tracker.

If you read paper books, a Kindle or another app, Books does not see that reading. A repeating reminder in Reminders ("Read 10 pages") or a paper log works; our printable habit tracker has a monthly grid you can fill in with page counts.

Tips that actually help

  1. Set a minimum, not a target. Ten pages is the floor. On good days you will read 40, and that is a bonus, not the new rule.
  2. Attach it to an anchor. "After I get into bed, I will read 10 pages" is a plan; "read more" is not. This is habit stacking.
  3. Keep the book where the anchor is. On your pillow, in your bag, next to the kettle.
  4. Give yourself permission to quit a book. A book you dread stalls the whole habit. Start another.
  5. Be patient. Research suggests habits take weeks to months to feel automatic; see how long it takes to form a habit.

FAQ

How many pages a day to read 12 books a year?

About 10 pages a day, assuming books of around 300 pages: 12 × 300 = 3,600 pages, divided by 365 days.

How long does it take to read 10 pages?

Roughly 10 to 13 minutes at an average reading speed, based on average silent reading rates of about 238 to 260 words per minute and pages of about 250 to 300 words.

Should I track reading in pages or minutes?

Pages are easy to count and show progress through a book, while minutes treat dense and light books the same and suit audiobooks.

Can Apple Books track my reading habit?

Yes, Apple Books Reading Goals tracks daily reading, streaks and books read this year, but only for reading done in the Books app.
