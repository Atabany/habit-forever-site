About Habit Forever

Habit Forever is a small, independent app. One person designs it, builds it, answers the support email and writes the guides on this site.

Who makes it

I’m Mohamed Elatabany, an independent iOS developer. I build Habit Forever in Swift and SwiftUI and publish it on the App Store under my own name. There’s no company, investor or ad network behind it: the app is paid for by people who choose Habit Forever Pro.

I started it because I wanted a habit tracker that shows the shape of my days, not just a number. Every day you show up becomes a colored square, and over weeks those squares tell you more than any streak counter.

What the app stands for

  • Small and quick. Checking in should take a second. Adding a habit should take less than a minute.
  • Your data is yours. No account and no sign-in. Habits and notes are stored on your device, and Pro lets you export a backup file whenever you like.
  • Free that’s actually useful. Up to three habits with goals, reminders, notes, streaks and grids, with no time limit.
  • No ads. Not in the free version, not ever.

How the guides are written

Every guide is written by me. Claims about Habit Forever come from the app itself, and I update them when the app changes. Claims about iOS follow Apple’s own documentation. Research is cited with links to the original papers, not to summaries. When I compare apps, I disclose that I make Habit Forever and I say when another app is the better fit. Each page shows when it was last updated.

If something is wrong or out of date, email me at atabany.apps@gmail.com and I’ll correct it.

About the name

Until version 4.0 the app was listed on the App Store as “Habit Streak Tracker: Routines” and showed “Habit Tracker” on the Home Screen. It’s now Habit Forever, listed as “Habit Forever: Streak Tracker”. Same app, same developer, and your data carries over.

About Habit Forever

Habit Forever is a habit tracker for iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later) by independent developer Mohamed Elatabany. Track check-off or number habits with goals, reminders, notes, streaks and colorful day-by-day grids, plus Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and Siri Shortcuts. There is no account, and habit data stays on your device. It is free for up to three habits; Habit Forever Pro (yearly, monthly or a one-time lifetime purchase) adds unlimited habits, deeper insights, widgets for every habit and backup export/import. It is rated 5.0★ on the App Store, from 4 ratings, where it is listed as “Habit Forever: Streak Tracker” (formerly “Habit Streak Tracker: Routines”).
